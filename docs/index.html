Does local weather explain price surprises?

A "price surprise" is how far a month's price was from the usual level for that month. Each dot shows how strongly unusual rain or heat in the district lined up with surprises 0–3 months later (correlation, −1 to +1; beyond ±0.3 is worth a look).
